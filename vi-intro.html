Part 4

Vietnamese (Hán-Việt)

A large share of the Vietnamese vocabulary was borrowed from Chinese and is known as Hán-Việt (Sino-Vietnamese). Although modern Vietnamese is written in the Latin-based Quốc Ngữ alphabet, each Sino-Vietnamese morpheme corresponds to a Chinese character (chữ Hán / Hán tự) with a regular reading — just as Japanese on-yomi and Korean hanja readings do. This part groups those characters by phonetic component and orders them by the frequency of their readings, so that recognizing one character helps you guess the reading of its relatives. Each character is shown with its Hán-Việt reading(s), an English keyword, and example Sino-Vietnamese words written in Hán tự with their Quốc Ngữ spelling.
