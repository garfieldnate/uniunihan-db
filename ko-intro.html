Part 3

Korean (Educational Hanja)

This part covers the Basic Hanja for Educational Use (교육용 기초 한자) — the South Korean Ministry of Education's list of 1,800 hanja taught in secondary school. Each is grouped by phonetic component and ordered by the regularity of its hanja eum reading, shown with example Sino-Korean vocabulary.
