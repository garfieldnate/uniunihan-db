Part 2

Japanese (Jōyō kanji)

This part covers the Jōyō kanji (常用漢字) — the Japanese Ministry of Education's list of regular-use characters taught in schools (2,140 characters here). Each is grouped by phonetic component and ordered by the regularity of its on-yomi readings, shown with example vocabulary and cross-references to the same character in the other languages.
